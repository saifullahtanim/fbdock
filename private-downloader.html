Frequently Asked Questions

Where are videos saved after being downloaded?

Downloaded videos are saved directly to your device, usually in your default 'Downloads' folder. The exact location may vary depending on your browser, device, or download settings.

Why the video is playing instead of downloading?

That's something normal to happen, especially on browsers other than Chrome. To solve this issue, instead of left clicking the Download Video link, Right Click -> Save as... and choose the location you'd like to save the video to.

Does FBDock work on Mobile devices?

Currently FBDock works just fine with Android and iOS devices. Videos are usually saved in the 'Downloads' folder by default, you can then transfer them to your Photos app using a file manager. You can check our How to Download Facebook Videos on iPhone or iPad guide.

Can I download Live Facebook videos?

Yes, you can download Facebook Live videos but only after they finish streaming.

Video with no audio/only audio

Videos that contain Copyrighted Music are the most affected. We've found a solution to this, just Click "Video with No Audio" on the download page of your video and you'll be able to convert the video with audio.

Does FBDock store downloaded videos or keep a copy of videos?

FBDock doesn't store videos, nor do we keep copies of downloaded videos. All videos are hosted on Facebook's servers. Additionally, we do not track users’ download histories, helping ensure that your activity on our website remains anonymous.

Is FBDock free to use?

Yes, FBDock is completely free to use. You can access and use all of our features at no cost, with no subscriptions or unexpected charges.
