How to Download Facebook Videos

Fast & easy 3-step guide for any device

  • PC / Mac
  • iPhone / iPad / Android
Step 01

Copy the Facebook Video Link

Find the video you wish to download on your Facebook feed. Click the Share button underneath the post and select "Copy Link". Alternatively, right-click the video and choose "Copy video URL at current time".

Step 02

Paste the URL into FBDock

Go to FBDock, paste your copied link into the search box, and click the "Download" button. FBDock will process and extract the media streaming links in less than a second.

Step 03

Select Quality & Download

Choose your preferred download quality: click "Download in HD Quality" for maximum resolution, "Download in Normal Quality" for standard file size, or extract pure audio with "Force Audio Download".

Pro Tip: Video Plays Instead of Downloading?

If the video starts playing in your browser rather than downloading directly, simply right-click the video player and choose "Save Video As..." to save it directly to your computer.

Having Trouble with Private Videos?

Make sure the video privacy is set to Public. For closed groups or private posts, try our Private Video Downloader

Step 01

Copy Link from Facebook App

Open your Facebook mobile app. Tap the Share button beneath the video, then tap "Copy Link" from the share menu.

Step 02

Paste Link in Safari or Chrome

Open your mobile browser (Safari, Chrome), visit FBDock, paste your video link into the box, and tap "Download".

Step 03

Save Video to Your Device

Tap on HD Quality or Normal Quality. On iOS, tap "Download" when prompted. Your video will be automatically saved in your Files app → Downloads folder.

Finding Your Saved Videos on iPhone/iPad

Open the built-in Files app on your iPhone or iPad, tap iCloud Drive → Downloads (or On My iPhone → Downloads) to view or save to your Camera Roll.
